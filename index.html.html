About

I'm an illustrator working primarily in black and white pen and ink, heavily influenced by the bold linework and cross-hatching techniques of bronze age and modern age comics.

My work uses traditional hatching methods to create depth, texture, and dramatic contrast — a practice rooted in the great tradition of comic book and editorial illustration.

Influences

  • Alfredo Alcala
  • Moebius
  • Robert Crumb
  • Frank Miller
  • John Byrne
  • Mike Mignola
  • Brian Bolland
  • Carlos Ezquerra
